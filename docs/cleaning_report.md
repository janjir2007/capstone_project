## 5. Limits

- The noise is the generator's own (synthetic_rules §7). Real records will have problems it did not simulate (e.g. a wrong digit in a plate splits one truck into two; 1.4a saw this). Ari runs `python -m src.data.cleaning --input <real.csv> --output data/processed/<name>.csv --no-score` and shares the issue counts only.
- Destination aliases (`configs/cleaning.yaml`) were written separately from the generator's spellings, but by the same team; real spellings may be harder.
- Odometer checks need ≥ 2 readings of the same truck. A replaced dashboard (all later readings lower) would be flagged reading by reading, not as one reset.
